MSBA Capstone

Project Geminae — Induced Seismicity Causal Dashboard

Causal Inference
GitHub ↗

A multi-radius causal-inference pipeline that isolates how wastewater injection actually drives induced earthquakes near faults — not just correlates with them.

660K+ well records 6.2K+ seismic events 20× effect at 3–4km vs. 20km R² ≈ 0.55 at 5–7km

Problem

Regulators and well operators need to know whether injection volume causally drives seismic magnitude near faults, and at what distance risk actually concentrates — correlation alone can't justify regulation.

Approach

Spatially joined wells to seismic events across 1–20km radii, then built a DoWhy causal DAG (injection volume → pressure → magnitude, controlling for fault distance and fault-segment density) with bootstrap confidence intervals and refutation testing at both well- and event-level, surfaced through an interactive dashboard.

Impact

Found effects up to ~20× stronger at 3–4km than at 20km, and identified 5–7km as the radius that best balances effect size and predictive power — evidence for spatially-targeted rather than blanket regulation.

MSBA Coursework · Business Value Through Integrative Analytics

Horizon Telecom — Data-Driven Retention Strategy

Customer Retention

A three-stage descriptive → predictive → prescriptive pipeline that replaces reactive, one-size-fits-all retention with a revenue-at-risk-ranked, budget-constrained intervention plan.

$154K/week net value (+5.5%) 0.90 ROC-AUC 99.92% of optimal value in a simple playbook

Problem

The retention team only engaged customers after they initiated cancellation with last-ditch, non-personalized offers, while acquisition costs run 5–8× higher than retention and no early-warning system flagged high-value customers before they churned.

Approach

Segmented 7,043 customers into 4 behavioral clusters via K-means, built a signal-leakage-audited logistic regression churn model (removing 16 leaky variables), computed expected value per customer-tier combination (v = p̂ × η × CLTV − cost), and solved a multi-tier integer program allocating 150 weekly contacts under a $2,500 budget — validated with sensitivity analysis and a proposed A/B test to measure true intervention effectiveness.

Impact

The optimized multi-tier plan retains $154K in weekly net value vs. $146K from a uniform single-tier approach (+$8K, +5.5%), and a simplified decision-tree playbook recovers 99.92% of that value for one-click field deployment without a live solver.

MSBA Coursework · Optimization for Prescriptive Analytics

Airport Gate Allocation Optimization

Operations Research

A mixed-integer program that reassigns arrival and departure gates to cut passenger walking distance without breaking airline terminal preferences.

15.8% less walking distance 27 flights × 37 gates solved in <1 second

Problem

Manually built gate schedules waste passenger walking time and largely ignore airline terminal preferences, while still needing to respect fixed gate-occupancy windows and limited flight-time flexibility.

Approach

Formulated the problem as a mixed-integer program in Python with Gurobi: binary variables for arrival gate-and-time assignment and departure time-shift, a hierarchical multi-objective (minimize arrival delay, then departure shift, then passenger walking distance plus terminal-preference violations, then terminal balance), and passenger-level walking-cost matrices built from a full gate-to-gate travel-time table.

Impact

Solved a 1,917-constraint, 4,353-variable MIP in well under a second and cut total passenger walking distance by 15.8% (14,524 → 12,233.5 minutes) versus the manual baseline across 27 arrivals and 37 gates, with zero duplicate gate assignments.

MSBA Coursework · Analytical Marketing

Release-Date Optimization for The Maze Runner

Marketing Analytics

Topic-modeled movie release scheduling that finds low-competition weeks by measuring thematic similarity, not just shared genre labels.

10 latent topics (LDA) 4 low-competition weeks found 0.057 closest-competitor distance

Problem

Release-date scheduling usually relies on genre labels, which treat all same-genre films as equally competitive and miss the thematic overlap that actually drives audience cannibalization.

Approach

Combined MovieLens user tags with Opus movie metadata, ran Latent Dirichlet Allocation (K=10 topics) on user tags to build a content-based topic profile per film, measured similarity as Euclidean distance in topic space, and built a weekly hybrid dissimilarity score (0.7 × closest competitor + 0.3 × mean distance) across 2014 — validated against a genre-based K-means + PCA baseline.

Impact

Identified four low-competition release windows in 2014 (May 5, May 19, June 9, Nov 3) and the film's closest thematic substitutes (28 Weeks Later at distance 0.057, Resident Evil: Extinction, The Conjuring), recommending the week of June 9 with a positioning strategy built around minimal thematic overlap.

MSBA Coursework · Advanced AI and Business

Do Great Players Make Their Teammates Better?

Sports Analytics

An on/off-court statistical study of whether LeBron James's floor presence measurably improves teammate shooting efficiency.

+3.36% teammate FG% on-court p = 0.0465 37.6% of makes assisted by LeBron

Problem

It's widely assumed that superstar players make teammates better, but that claim is rarely tested statistically rather than anecdotally, and needs to separate a real on-court effect from confounds like lineup composition and opponent strength.

Approach

Parsed 2018–19 NBA play-by-play lineup data restricted to LeBron James's games, split teammates' shot attempts into on-court vs. off-court conditions within the same games to control for opponent, compared FG%, effective FG%, and points per shot attempt, ran two-proportion z-tests for significance, and checked the mechanism via assist rate and playmaking "gravity."

Impact

Teammates shot 48.4% from the field with LeBron on the floor vs. 45.0% off it (+3.36 points, statistically significant at p = 0.0465), scored 1.245 vs. 1.201 points per shot attempt, and had 37.6% of their makes assisted by him — a lift that ranked 6th among a peer group of other elite, high-usage stars.

PythonPandasNBA Play-by-Play DataTwo-Proportion Z-Test
